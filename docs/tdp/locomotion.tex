% --- \section{Introduction}: one sentence -----------------------------------
For locomotion, a single learned policy walks the robot over the whole course,
using a height map of the terrain built from the organizers' CAD.

% --- \section{System Description} ------------------------------------------
\subsection{Motion Planning and Whole-Body-Control}
Locomotion is one reinforcement-learning policy (PPO~\cite{schulman2017ppo})
that sends joint position targets to all 29 G1 joints at 50\,Hz. It is trained
in GPU simulation with mjlab~\cite{mjlab}, starting from Unitree's open-source
G1 tasks~\cite{unitree_rl_mjlab}. To these we add: a 1.6$\times$1.0\,m height
map at 5\,cm around the robot, taken from a CAD-based map of the course placed
by our localization; a pelvis-height command to crouch for floor-level buttons;
random arm motions during training, so the legs stay balanced while the IK
controller moves the arms; and training terrain sized to the measured course
(20.3\,cm stairs, 10\,cm beams, 15$^\circ$ ramps), including a MuJoCo digital
twin of the testbed. The operator steers it with walking-velocity and height
commands.

% --- \section{Competition Strategy}: mobility, one sentence -----------------
We attempt all four mobility tasks with this policy; to press a button, the
robot stops, crouches if needed, and the IK controller uses the arms while the
policy keeps balance.

% --- \section{Experiments}: one sentence -------------------------------------
In simulation, the digital twin matches the CAD to within 1.3\,mm and a
flat-ground walking policy works; the terrain-aware policy is in training.
